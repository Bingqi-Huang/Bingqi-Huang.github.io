About

I am a Ph.D. student in the School of Aerospace Engineering at Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Zhaokui Wang. I lead robotics research and development at the Distributed and Intelligent Space System Laboratory (DSSL), spanning learning algorithms, robot platforms, and real-world deployment.

02 / Robotic systems

LunarBot / Research platform
Principal engineer2024–Present

LunarBot

A holonomic mobile manipulation platform for robotics research.

I led its end-to-end development, from mechanical and electrical design to assembly, ROS software, system integration, and debugging.

  • Hardware4-wheel independent steering and driving; RealMan RM75 manipulator.
  • ControlQP-based whole-body control for coordinated mobile manipulation.
  • LearningLeRobot-format data collection, π0.5 training and adaptation, and real-robot deployment.
